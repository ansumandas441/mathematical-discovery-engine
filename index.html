What is this?

Most of mathematics is recorded as prose — theorems, proofs, and the techniques behind them scattered across centuries of papers. The Mathematical Discovery Engine encodes that web as a single graph: every state (a known result or object), every axiom, every theorem, and the technique edges that move you from one to the next.

Once proof is a graph, discovery becomes search. Finding a new proof is finding a path; finding an open problem is finding a promising node with no outgoing edge yet. The graph is LLM-orchestrated — an automated pipeline ingests mathematical sources, deduplicates against the canon, and grows the structure.

Everything here is open source. Spin the graph, follow a chain of techniques, and see how Fourier connects to number theory connects to topology. Read the full writeup on GitHub →
